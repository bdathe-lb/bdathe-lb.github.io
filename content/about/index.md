Hello, I am **Bdathe**.

I am currently a graduate student in Computer Science and Technology, focusing on **UAV Task Allocation** and **Path Planning**.

### Why this site?

Throughout my journey of studying and coding, I realized that knowledge needs to be consolidated, as fragmented memories easily fade away. Therefore, I built this "digital garden" to organize my thoughts and document my growth.

### What's here?

* **文 / writing**:
    Footprints along the timeline — essays, toy projects, debugging logs, and study notes.

* **诗 / poems**:
    A few poems worth coming back to, with a plain-language reading of each.

* **音 / music**:
    A collection of songs that resonate with me.

* **影 / photos**:
    Beyond code, I also love observing the world. Here lies the scenery and life moments captured through my lens.

### Final Thoughts

As an explorer still on the road, my understanding and technical skills are constantly iterating. Naturally, the content here will be updated or corrected as my learning progresses.

Aware that I am still climbing the steep learning curve of technology, I am actively working to bridge my knowledge gaps. You can find some practice projects on my GitHub — most of them are replications of classic tools or architectures (reinventing the wheel).

I believe in the saying:

<blockquote class="about-quote">
  "What I cannot create, I do not understand."
  <cite>— Richard Feynman</cite>
</blockquote>

Due to my limited expertise, errors or omissions are inevitable. Corrections via email are most welcome.

I hope we can all find joy in the ocean of technology.
