{{/* 霞鹜文楷. The jsDelivr package is already split by unicode-range, so a visitor only downloads the subsets their page actually uses — that is the whole reason we can afford a CJK webfont here. Loaded as rel=preload rather than the media="print" trick: both fetch without blocking render, but print media is fetched at the *lowest* priority, which put the swap well after the page had settled. On a cross-document view transition that meant the text visibly re-set itself a beat after the new page had already faded in. Preload asks for it at stylesheet priority instead, so the swap usually lands before or during the transition rather than after it. The shift the swap causes is dealt with in main.css — see the 'WenKai Fallback' faces, whose metrics are overridden to match 文楷. To self-host later: npm i lxgw-wenkai-screen-webfont, copy the woff2 + style.css into static/fonts/, and point this at it. Running the family through cn-font-split first gives finer subsets still. */}}
